The Bucharest Landmarks Paper Cup

Take a piece of Bucharest's history home. A paper cup printed with ink drawings of the city's landmarks, and a kraft sleeve you turn to reveal each one.

The idea

Most cups end up in the bin. This one comes home with you.

The cup is printed all the way around with four ink drawings of Bucharest landmarks. A kraft sleeve with an arched window fits snugly around it. Turn the sleeve, and a different piece of the city appears in the frame. The cup becomes a small gallery of the capital that you can hold, collect and keep.

The landmarks

Four places that tell the story of Bucharest.

Each landmark on The Bucharest Landmarks Paper Cup is a detailed ink drawing, chosen for what it says about the city. Together they make a portrait of culture, power, memory and tradition.

The cultural heart

Romanian Athenaeum

Since it opened in 1888, the Athenaeum has been the concert hall where Bucharest listens to itself. For many people it is the most graceful building in the city, and a symbol of Romanian culture.

In the drawing, its round dome rises above a row of tall columns, a calm and perfectly balanced picture that opens the collection with elegance.

The monumental

Palace of the Parliament

One of the largest administrative buildings in the world, it is impossible to talk about modern Bucharest without it. It stands for scale, ambition and a complicated chapter that the city has made its own.

On the cup, its wide, layered facade gives the collection its most dramatic scene, a grounded horizontal beside the dome and the arch.

The triumphal arch

Arcul de Triumf

The arch that stands today was inaugurated in 1936 and honours the soldiers and the union of the Romanian lands. It is a landmark of national pride, framed by the green avenues of the city's north.

It is also the perfect companion for the sleeve: its arch echoes the arched window, so the landmark appears to be framed by its own shape.

The hidden gem

Stavropoleos Church

Built in 1724, this small church is tucked into the Old Town, right where the city's cafés and streets are liveliest. It is loved for its carved stone details and its arcaded porch.

The drawing captures its domed tower and graceful arches, an intimate scene that reminds you that Bucharest's history is often found in its smallest corners.
