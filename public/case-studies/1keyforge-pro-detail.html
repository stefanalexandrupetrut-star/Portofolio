1KEYFORGE PRO

Overview

A single-file mobile web app for designing a key from its bitting code. Choose one of eight profiles, move a slider for each pin and the key redraws at true scale with a milled V-cut at every position. The same outline exports as an SVG for drawing, an STL for 3D printing or a DXF for CNC and laser. The case study covers the build and the geometry audit behind it.

Closing Notes

A small tool with a hard core: the interface is a handful of sliders, and the work is in making one outline that the preview, the STL and the DXF all share.

The key dimensions are working approximations of common profiles. The app is for visualising, learning and prototyping, not for copying a key you are not authorised to duplicate.
