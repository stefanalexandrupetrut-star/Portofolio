Spiderbot Arena

Overview

A competitive mode where players pilot a mechanical spider drone. I designed the flow end to end: finding and entering a match through the Online menu's Game Modes tab, the in-match HUD (a position/timer module that pulses in the final 15 seconds, plus a weapon/power-up module), the post-match flow (a 5-second “You Placed 1st” celebration into an 8-player scoreboard, then a reward screen for currency, items, and skins), and the loading screens shown between stages.
