URBAN STRIFE

UI/UX Design · MicroProse × White Pond Games
Overview

Full UI redesign of an isometric, Jagged Alliance-style tactical survival RPG — a diegetic, hardware-inspired interface of CRT readouts, ballistic calculators and field journals, from the combat HUD to inventory, shelter, crafting, trade and map. Done remotely, reworking every HUD, menu and widget while keeping the retro, old-school turn-based, post-apocalyptic identity at the heart of the game.

Closing Notes

Modernizing a retro identity isn't a polish pass. The hard part was deciding what not to change, so the game stayed true to its old-school TBS roots.

A gritty, physical look only scales across dozens of screens when it sits on consistent templates and grids.
