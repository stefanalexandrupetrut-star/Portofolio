CHIBICHUM

Personal Project · Independent Game
Overview

A retro, cozy virtual pet that lives on your Windows desktop taskbar, web browser, or mobile device. ChibiChum is an 8-bit, Tamagotchi-style care game built to sit beside everyday work: a pet that roams its room, asks for care through gentle nudges, and rewards a few seconds of attention at a time. The case study covers the product vision, the game systems, the shared core engine behind the Windows, web and mobile builds, and the lessons learned along the way.

Closing Notes

ChibiChum takes a familiar idea, the virtual pet, and fits it to a modern day: small enough to live in a taskbar, rich enough to carry a care loop, minigames, a shop and a seasonal room, and built to reach three platforms from one core.

Three lessons carried through the build: pixel-art panels need slice-aware scaling, the pet’s behavior works best as a state machine, and an always-on window is judged on its idle cost.
